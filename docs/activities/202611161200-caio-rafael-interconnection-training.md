# First-contact and interconnection training

Updated `sufficit-telephony-dialplan` to 0.7.2 with [first-contact guidance](../../skills/sufficit/sufficit-telephony-dialplan/references/first-contact.md) and a carrier-comparison procedure. A passing internal test does not establish reachability from every carrier or prove that a customer's phone rings. The guidance includes progressive customer follow-up, resource-context/authorization checks and private manager coordination for mobile-origin tests with each carrier reported explicitly.

The role-specific text was applied through supported training-management interfaces and read back. No WhatsApp request or DID test was sent as part of this training update. Sending capability and actual delivery are not implied by training text.

The package version, catalog, card and manifest digest were aligned. All 193 public skill packages passed validation in an isolated task snapshot. Operational receipts, internal identifiers and case evidence are kept in private deployment records.
