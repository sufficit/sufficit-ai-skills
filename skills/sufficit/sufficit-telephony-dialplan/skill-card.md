# sufficit-telephony-dialplan

Design and manage Sufficit Telephony call routing: attendance flows, applications and destinations, queues and members, voicemail as a destination, inbound DID numbers and outbound trunk selection, through the live corporate API and the product semantics. Use when a request mentions fluxo de atendimento, aplicacoes e destinos, filas, URA, IVR, tronco, DID, numero de entrada, horario de atendimento or caixa postal como destino. Not for editing raw Asterisk extensions.conf.
