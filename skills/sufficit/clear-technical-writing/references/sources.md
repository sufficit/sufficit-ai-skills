# Sources and limits

The ASD-STE100 is a technical writing standard in English. It combines writing rules with a controlled dictionary and originated from aeronautical maintenance documentation. The principle of reducing variations in terms helps to avoid ambiguity.

## Primary sources

- [Official site of STEMG](https://www.asd-ste100.org/): locate available edition and official means of access to the standard.
- [FAQ official](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html): explains rules, dictionary and use of words with controlled meaning.
- [About STE](https://www.asd-ste100.org/about_STE.html): purpose and context.

The sources were identified during research on 2026-10-16. The indexed official results indicated Issue 9 (2025); the direct opening of FAQ and About STE returned HTTP 403. The full text of the standard has not been incorporated or validated in this skill. Check the required edition by the project before a normative review.

## Review against the standard

Use the edition specified by the user or the document contract. Without specified edition, check the one made available by the official source and report which one you used. Examine the rules applicable to the type of text, the use and meaning of words in the dictionary and the processing of the technical terms of the domain itself.

When access is blocked, do not replace the standard with memory or a third-party summary. To complete the normative review, request access to the applicable material; meanwhile, limit the delivery to the possible editorial review. An automated checker can help locate problems, but does not alone prove that the document meets all the requirements.
